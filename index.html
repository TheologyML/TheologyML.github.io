About TML

See the ideas behind the ideas.

TML is a visual modeling language made for the work of understanding.

It gives theology, biblical studies, church history, philosophy, historical analysis, and knowledge organization a shared visual grammar. Each diagram represents concepts, claims, sources, and the relationships between them.

Use TML to place a doctrine alongside its scriptural foundations, trace the development of an idea through history, or bring a dense research project into one navigable view.

Why TML exists

Complex thought deserves a clearer surface.

Theological systems grow dense quickly: concepts overlap, sources interpret one another, and arguments unfold across centuries. Ordinary notes preserve information, but often leave the connections invisible.

Existing diagram tools were built for software engineering. TML was created to model theological systems on their own terms—so you can discover relationships that are difficult to recognize in plain text alone.
